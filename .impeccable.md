## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, and other developers reviewing Kavya Nagariya's portfolio. They need to quickly understand skills, experience, and view projects in a memorable but frictionless way.

### Brand Personality
Cinematic, Dark Fantasy, Mysterious, Refined. (Thematic inspiration: Elden Ring / "The Tarnished Archive")

### Aesthetic Direction
Extreme Minimal.
- **Visuals**: Focus on raw typography, generous dark space, and the removal of explicit borders or "card" containers.
- **Interactions**: Restrained. No complex 3D hover effects. Rely on subtle opacity fades, smooth scrolling, and elegant typography transitions.
- **Unique Elements**: A custom thematic cursor and an atmospheric "mist" background effect to ground the dark fantasy theme without cluttering the UI.

### Design Principles
1. **Typography Over Containers**: Use space, font size, and contrast to group information, not boxes.
2. **Restrained Motion**: Animations should feel like slowly dissipating fog or embers, not snappy app interactions.
3. **Atmospheric Depth**: Achieve depth through the background (video + mist) rather than foreground elements (shadows/3D transforms).
4. **Cinematic Polish**: Every element should feel intentional and cohesive, avoiding generic UI patterns.
